Tiny Wins - ambient sound sources and licences
Audit date: 18 Oct 2026 (rain re-processed and lofi sources added 19 Oct 2026). Each source page was re-opened and the licence text read on that date.

The files in this folder are PROCESSED COPIES (mono, 48 kbps mp3, filtered, loudness-matched, crossfaded into seamless loops).
The app uses the base64 copies in ../sounds.js. CC0 and public-domain works need no attribution; credit is given anyway.

Lofi player (in-app, ONLINE ONLY, streamed - not bundled, not cached)
   The app streams nine chill instrumental tracks by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com) with an HTML <audio> element from Wikimedia Commons (https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/...). Nothing is downloaded into the app, stored in the service-worker cache, or saved on the device by the app.
   Licence:  Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0) https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/ - as stated on each Commons file page ("This file is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported license"; "Attribution: <Title> Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com)"). Conditions: give appropriate credit, link to the licence, indicate if changes were made, do not suggest endorsement.
   Author's terms: https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/faq.html says the music may be used on streaming sites and in other projects, and to "be sure to credit me". It says a credit must be easy to find, and that a reasonable effort such as clicking on a credits option is acceptable; for video games it names a Credits screen in the settings menu. The author's site now offers the same music under CC BY 4.0; the Commons copies we stream are labelled CC BY 3.0, so the app credits CC BY 3.0 with a link to the licence text.
   Hotlinking: Wikimedia's policy allows embedding/hotlinking files from Wikimedia servers (it is "not generally recommended" for reliability reasons). The app therefore fails over to the next track when a file does not load and shows a message when all fail.
   Changes:  none to the audio. The app plays the files exactly as published; it only plays them quieter (a per-track volume factor) so all tracks sound equally loud.
   Credit display: Settings > Credits lists every track (title, artist, source link, track-info link, licence link, changes) and all sound credits. The Focus screen shows no credit text, only the title of the track that is playing; the system media controls show title and artist.
